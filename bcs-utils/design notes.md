# PAYLOADS

The spine of course is the main payload, but also included are secondary and tertiary weapons, defenses, and marines.  Total these values to determine the total payload tonnage.

## Secondaries, Tertiaries, and Marines

Determine the Secondary offense rating.  Secondary offenses are most often bay weapons. Use the Secondary Tonnage-Rating table below.

# PERCENTAGE SYSTEMS

## Essentials

Bridge Volume. 2%.
Crew Life Support Volume. 6%.

## Primary Defense

Determine the Primary Defense rating.  The rule is 1 point of defense for every 4% tons of ship tonnage. 

## Drives

Jump Volume. 2.5% per jump number. 10% fuel per jump number.
Hop Volume. 2.5% per hop number. 10% fuel per hop number.
Maneuver Volume. 1% per G of acceleration.
Power Volume. 1.5% per number. Fuel volume depends on tech level:
* TL 15+: 1% per number
* TL 13-14: 2% per number
* TL 9-12: 3% per number
* TL 7-8: 4% per number

## SUBTOTAL PERCENTAGE

Add all percentage systems percentages together to get a subtotal percentage.


# DETERMINE VOLUME

Use the following formula to determine total volume:

	Total Volume = Subtotal Payload / (1 - Subtotal Percentage)

Where:
* Total Volume = Total ship volume in tons
* Subtotal Payload = Total payload tonnage in tons
* Subtotal Percentage = Total percentage systems as a decimal (e.g., 25% = 0.25)
